# Storyboard — provisional clock before accepted voice

| Segment | Picture / business job | Exact key type | Transition / sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 ~0–9s | S01 candid uncertain buyer; relatable purchase | همه می‌گفتن عالیه… / برای تو چی؟ | Close push, paper flick, title closes like parcel edge |
| 02 ~9–19s | S02 parcels overhead; uncertainty becomes a preference | اول، نیاز تو | Rectangular parcel edge becomes choice frame; 1 restrained air sweep |
| 03 ~19–27s | Choice 1 occupies frame; preferences are choices, no score | چی برات مهم‌تره؟ / نتیجه، کیفیت، قیمت | Selected row becomes persistent brass rail. Pause after narration. |
| 04 ~27–37s | Choice 2 with cutout magnifier; how customer wants information | اول چطور بررسی می‌کنی؟ / امتحان، تجربه، تفاوت | Rail opens second choice; soft click only on actual selection. Pause. |
| 05 ~37–48s | Typography choreography: product first vs need first, S03 texture detail | اول نیازت / بعد بررسی محصول / بعد تصمیم تو | Ordered words exchange position; brass edge tracks transition; understated accent |
| 06 ~48–61s | S04 conversation; no promised outcome | یه گفت‌وگوی کوتاه | Two selected rows form request summary, warm light reveal |
| 07 ~61–75s | Clear consultation invitation; form opens on real click | نیاز به مشاوره دارم | Request button expands into 2-field sheet, clean click, music resolves |

Three signature transformations: parcel edge → choice frame; selected row → persistent summary rail; CTA button → consultation form. Distinct macro/human/overhead/type/choice/form compositions, at least 16 picture/type layouts over final voice clock. No text over faces, no glyph-by-glyph Persian split, no unnecessary flashes. User can choose at own speed; voice pauses at question boundaries. Form: name + mobile, purpose notice; submit once and handle retry without duplicate row. No fake confirmation.
